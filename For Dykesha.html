Dear Dykesha,

I don't say this often enough, so let me start here: being around you is my favorite part of any day. Not the big, planned-out moments — just the ordinary ones. You walking in and telling me about your day before you've even sat down. The way you laugh before the joke is finished. Those are the parts I hold onto.

You have a way of making hard things feel lighter, just by being there. I don't think you always notice you're doing it. It's in how you listen — actually listen, not just wait for your turn to talk. It's in how you remember the small things I mention once and never bring up again, and then you bring them up.

I'm grateful for your patience on the days I'm not my best — the tired ones, the distracted ones, the ones where I don't explain myself well. You give me room to be human, and you never make me feel small for it. That's rarer than people think, and I don't take it for granted.

You also make me laugh more than almost anyone I know — the ridiculous kind of laughing, over nothing in particular, the kind that sneaks up on both of us at the worst possible time. I hope we never lose that. It's one of my favorite things about us.

There's a steadiness in you that I've come to rely on without ever meaning to. Life gets loud sometimes, and you're the quiet in it. I don't know exactly how to thank someone for that, other than to keep showing up the same way for you.

So this is my long way of saying: thank you for choosing this, choosing us, on the ordinary days as much as the good ones. I don't have a bigger occasion for saying it — I just wanted you to have it in writing, somewhere you can come back to whenever you need a reminder.

You are so easy to love, I hope I remind you of that as often as you deserve.

With everything I've got,

yours

a thousand small moments
made with love, just for you
